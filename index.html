STAY PROTECTED

Stay safe while using the internet

🔐

Never share OTPs

Do not share OTPs, PINs or passwords with callers or strangers.

🌐

Verify website addresses

Use trusted official links. A .gov.in address alone is not enough; check the exact domain.

⚠️

Avoid suspicious links

Do not click unexpected messages promising prizes or urgent benefits.

Suspect a cyber financial fraud? In India, call 1930 promptly and report it at cybercrime.gov.in.
